---
title: "Configure your instance"
---

AUTH_URL is the public HTTPS address of your application. Use a unique AUTH_SECRET for each installation. Database data and uploads need persistent storage.


```text
AUTH_URL=https://inventory.example.com
AUTH_SECRET=replace-with-a-long-random-secret
AUTH_TRUST_HOST=true
```

- Files: the local upload volume or a configured Openinary instance.

- AI: optional for image analysis, counting, translations and covers. Organization models and defaults live under Settings → AI.

- Maps: default maps load external tiles; you can configure your own compatible tile URLs.
